Career journey

Every stop sharpened the signal

  1. 2009
    Nokia · Lisbon, PT GDC Network Integration Engineer

    "First day. Cold server room. Cables everywhere. This is where it starts."

    Gained

    2G and 3G integration from scratch. Physical network understanding most engineers never touch.

    Why move

    Mastered the baseline. The world was bigger than Lisbon.

  2. 2013
    O2 / Telefónica · London, UK NMC Radio Analyst

    "London. Millions of users. National scale. This is the real thing."

    Gained

    Live ops at national scale. KPI discipline. BT, Vodafone, major partners. International confidence.

    Why move

    Wanted to go deeper. Below radio. Below everything.

  3. 2018
    Alcatel-Lucent · London, UK Network Design Engineer

    "Submarine cables. Under the ocean. Connecting continents. How is this even a job?"

    Gained

    The deepest layer of global internet infrastructure. Optical systems. Expertise very few engineers ever touch.

    Why move

    5G was coming. Wanted to build it.

  4. 2021
    Nokia · Lisbon, PT Network Design & Planning Engineer

    "5G. Not talking about it. Building it."

    Gained

    End-to-end 5G RAN design. Parameter tuning. Frequency planning. The complete modern mobile picture.

    Why move

    Networks are moving to the cloud. I needed to move with them.

  5. 2024
    Genesis Technologies · UK Data Centre & GDC Engineer

    "The cloud has to live somewhere. I'm going to learn every rack it lives in."

    Gained

    Physical cloud infrastructure. VMO2 and 3UK enterprise deployments. The bridge between telecom and cloud.

    Next

    AWS Solutions Architect Associate. The chapter that makes all the others make sense.
